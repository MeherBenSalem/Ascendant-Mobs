# Remnants: RPG Mobs 3.1.0 (draft, unreleased)

## New
- **Difficulty engine** (`difficulty.json`): difficulty grows with in-game days, your own play time, distance from
  spawn, depth and your gear, each on a tunable curve, per dimension. It feeds the levels and ranks you already
  know: harder places spawn higher levels and likelier Elites, Champions and Remnants.
- **Event nights** (`events.json`): the **Grim Vigil**, a night when named, ranked hunters track every survivor in
  waves and a Remnant-rank mini-boss comes last. See the dawn for experience, Valor and a new advancement. Add your
  own nights with their own schedule, waves and rewards.
- `/remnants difficulty [player]` and `/remnants event [list|start|stop]`.
- New entity tags `#remnant_mobs:ignores_difficulty`, `#remnant_mobs:event_hunters`,
  `#remnant_mobs:event_mini_bosses` and dimension type tag `#remnant_mobs:no_difficulty`.
- API: `getDifficulty`, `getActiveEvent`, `registerBossTier`.
- New messages fall back to English text on clients without the mod's language file.

## Upgrading
**Nothing changes until you opt in.** Remnants finds your existing config and creates `difficulty.json` and
`events.json` with every new system switched off. Set `enabled: true` in either and run `/remnants reload`.
Fresh installs start with them on. Levels, ranks, traits, rewards and the Hunter's Moon behave exactly as in 3.0
either way; the five 3.0 config files are not rewritten and world data keeps its format.
